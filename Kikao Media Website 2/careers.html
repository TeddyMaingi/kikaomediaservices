CAREERS & TRAINING

Learn by doing. Build by creating.

Kikao Media Services is committed to creating opportunities for young people who want to build practical skills in media, communication and digital content.

Our training and attachment opportunities give students and emerging creatives the opportunity to work in a real media environment and learn through practical production.

01

Student Attachment

We provide students with opportunities to gain practical experience alongside their academic studies.

  • Radio & broadcasting
  • Video production
  • Digital content
  • Social media
  • Production support
02

Media Apprenticeships

Our apprenticeship opportunities are designed for young people who want hands-on experience in the media industry.

  • Content production
  • Research
  • Editing
  • Broadcast production
  • Digital publishing
03

New Media Training

We expose emerging creatives to the practical skills required to work in today's digital media environment.

  • Digital storytelling
  • Podcast production
  • Social media content
  • Video production
  • Audience engagement

OUR APPROACH

The best way to learn media is to make media.

We believe young media professionals learn best when they are given responsibility, exposed to real projects and allowed to work alongside experienced team members.

At Kikao, trainees are encouraged to ask questions, experiment, create and develop the discipline required to work in a professional media environment.
