WHO WE ARE

We create media that helps organizations communicate, connect and grow.

Kikao Media is a Kenyan digital media and production company helping organizations turn ideas, information and stories into media that people can understand, watch and engage with.

We combine media, storytelling and production to help organizations communicate more effectively with the audiences that matter to them.

Whether you need to explain an important issue, tell your organization's story, create engaging digital content or reach a wider audience, we provide the creative and production support to make it happen.

WHAT WE DO

One media partner. Multiple ways to communicate.

01

Communication & Education Partner

We help organizations communicate information in ways that are clear, useful and easy to understand.

From public education and awareness campaigns to expert discussions and educational content, we turn complex ideas into communication that people can connect with.

THE VALUE

Your message becomes easier to understand, easier to share and more likely to reach the people who need it.

02

Media & Production Partner

We provide the production capability organizations need to tell their stories professionally.

Our work includes brand content, documentaries, interviews, podcasts, event coverage, livestreaming and digital video production.

THE VALUE

You get professional media production without having to build and manage a full production team internally.

03

Media Distribution & Audience Engagement Partner

We don't believe content should simply sit on a hard drive after production.

Through Kikao's digital media platforms and content ecosystem, we create opportunities for organizations to take their stories further and connect with audiences through relevant digital channels.

THE VALUE

Your content has a better opportunity to reach people, generate conversations and extend the life of your communication.

OUR MEDIA

We don't just produce media. We build it.

Kikao operates its own digital media platforms and produces original content focused on the issues, ideas and stories that matter to Kenyan audiences.

Our in-house work includes digital shows, interviews, discussions, podcasts, documentaries and online radio.

This gives us something important: we understand media from both sides.

We understand what it takes to create content, but we also understand what makes audiences watch, listen, share and engage.

That experience informs the work we do for our clients.
