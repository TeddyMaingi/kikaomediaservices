KIKAO MEDIA SERVICES

Media that moves people.

We help organizations communicate their ideas, tell their stories and connect with the audiences that matter.

Kikao Media Services is a Kenyan media, content and production organization built around one simple idea: good communication should do more than make people see or hear a message. It should make people understand, engage and respond.

Through digital media, video production, storytelling, live production, documentaries, radio, podcasts and branded content, we help organizations turn their ideas and objectives into communication that people can connect with.

OUR MEDIA

We don't just produce content. We build platforms for conversation.

Kikao Media Services operates across digital media, radio and video production. Our platforms give us an understanding of how audiences consume information, what makes people pay attention and how stories can move from a conversation into meaningful engagement.

Our content spans public affairs, society, culture, business, health, law and everyday issues affecting Kenyans.

This gives our clients more than production capacity. It gives them access to a media environment where their ideas, stories and messages can be developed for real audiences.
